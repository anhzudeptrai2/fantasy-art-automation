Recovery rules shown above

    

      
Refresh before expiryTokens are renewed 60 s early. The rotated refresh token is saved; a failed refresh keeps the old one.

      
Back off on errorsEtsy 5xx: wait 0.5 s, 1 s, 2 s between attempts, up to 4 attempts.

      
Respect rate limitsEtsy 429: wait exactly as long as its Retry-After header says.

      
Know when to stopA revoked login is never retried. Persistent failure raises an alert for an operator.
